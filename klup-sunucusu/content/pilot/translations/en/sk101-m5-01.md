# What do accounts and groups determine on Windows?

> Editorial draft. Awaiting technical and language review; this is not published pilot content.

## Incident

Instead of an account's name, look at the groups it belongs to and the rights it has been granted. Membership of the administrators group is not an automatic requirement for everyday work. Separate the local account from the corporate identity; make changes through the authorised management process. Don't declare an account trustworthy just by looking at the name in a screenshot.

## Synthetic evidence

```text
Account: lab-reader
Local groups: Users
Task: read synthetic reports
```

## Review steps

1. Determine the account scope
2. Review the groups and rights
3. Compare with the task requirement

Key concept: **group**.

## Remember

- The lowest rights the task requires should be the baseline.
- Separate observations from assumptions.
- Record the evidence, the scope and the reason for the next step.
