# Where does permission start and end?

> Editorial draft. Technical and language review are pending; this is not published pilot content.

## The case

The permission the Club gives you must contain a target, a time window and a list of methods. An interesting address outside the scope does not automatically become an authorised target. If anything is unclear, stop and ask the scope owner to explain. Do not replace training data with real personal data; pass the finding on through the organisation's approved reporting channel.

## Synthetic evidence

```text
Allowed: lab.mavifener.example
Window: 10:00-11:00
Excluded: production systems
```

## Investigation steps

1. Read the written permission
2. Compare the target and method with the scope
3. Ask the authorised person about any uncertainty

Key term: **scope**.

## Remember

- Only actions inside the written scope may be carried out.
- Keep observations separate from assumptions.
- Record the evidence, the scope and the reason for the next step.
