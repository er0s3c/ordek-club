# What context accompanies a Windows event ID?

> Editorial draft. Awaiting technical and language review; this is not published pilot content.

## Incident

4624 can be used for successful sign-in, 4625 for failed sign-in and 4720 for account creation events. The event source, audit setting, account and device context are still needed. Creating an account may also be an authorised administrative task. Compare the records on a timeline and verify them against the change record; don't draw a definite conclusion of a breach from the number alone.

## Synthetic evidence

```text
02:10 EventID=4625 Account=lab-admin
02:12 EventID=4624 Account=lab-admin
02:13 EventID=4720 Target=lab-temp
```

## Review steps

1. Verify the source and time information
2. Correlate account events
3. Compare with the change record

Key concept: **event**.

## Remember

- The 4720 line is an observation of account creation; the authorisation context is verified separately.
- Separate observations from assumptions.
- Record the evidence, the scope and the reason for the next step.
