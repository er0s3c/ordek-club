# What can we not infer from a process name?

> Editorial draft. Technical and language review are pending; this is not published pilot content.

## The case

A process name on its own does not prove that it is legitimate or that it is malicious. Examine the user, the parent process, the executable file and the timing together. Stopping the service carelessly can affect the evidence or business continuity. Record the observation first; justify the intervention through authority and impact.

## Synthetic evidence

```text
PID=421 USER=service COMMAND=worker
Parent=system service
Path=/opt/training/worker
```

## Investigation steps

1. Record the process context
2. Compare with the expected configuration
3. Justify the intervention

Key term: **process**.

## Remember

- The process identity and the execution context must be examined together.
- Keep observations separate from assumptions.
- Record the evidence, the scope and the reason for the next step.
