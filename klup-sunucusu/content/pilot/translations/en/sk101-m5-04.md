# What does a single service entry tell you?

> Editorial draft. Awaiting technical and language review; this is not published pilot content.

## Incident

Services and registry settings can affect how the system behaves. In a review, the executable path, the account and the start type matter as much as the service name. A new entry is not automatically malicious or legitimate. Compare it with the known configuration, preserve the evidence and report it without making unauthorised changes.

## Synthetic evidence

```text
Service: CampusReport
ImagePath: C:\Training\report.exe
Start: automatic
Change ticket: not yet checked
```

## Review steps

1. Record the service details
2. Find the expected configuration
3. Report the difference to the authorised owner

Key concept: **service**.

## Remember

- The path, the account and the change record should be assessed together.
- Separate observations from assumptions.
- Record the evidence, the scope and the reason for the next step.
