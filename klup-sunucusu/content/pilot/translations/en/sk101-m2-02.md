# What question do the address and port answer?

> Editorial draft. Technical and language review are pending; this is not published pilot content.

## The case

An IP address addresses an endpoint on the network, while a port helps you reach a service within the transport context. Different services can run on the same address. A port number alone proves neither the identity of the service nor its security. Read the fields in the training record separately, and do not confuse the connection result with the application result.

## Synthetic evidence

```text
Source: 192.0.2.12:51000
Destination: 192.0.2.40:443
TCP established
```

## Investigation steps

1. Determine the destination address
2. Determine the destination port
3. Verify the service with application evidence

Key term: **port**.

## Remember

- A port number alone is not proof of a secure service.
- Keep observations separate from assumptions.
- Record the evidence, the scope and the reason for the next step.
