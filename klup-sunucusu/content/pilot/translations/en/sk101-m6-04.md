# Why isn't a fast hash enough for storing passwords?

> Editorial draft. Awaiting technical and language review; this is not published pilot content.

## Incident

Use a derivation method designed for passwords, with an adjustable cost, and a unique salt for each password. A fast general-purpose digest on its own doesn't make password attempts expensive. The method and the cost are chosen according to current guidance and system capacity. A password is never written to a log or report in plain text.

## Synthetic evidence

```text
Option A: sha256(password)
Option B: approved_password_kdf(password, unique_salt, tuned_cost)
```

## Review steps

1. Identify the password storage method
2. Examine the salt and the cost
3. Verify against current guidance

Key concept: **salt**.

## Remember

- A derivation method suitable for passwords needs a unique salt and a measured cost.
- Separate observations from assumptions.
- Record the evidence, the scope and the reason for the next step.
