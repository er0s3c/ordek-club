# Who do we report a finding to, and how?

> Editorial draft. Technical and language review are pending. This is not published pilot content.

## The case

A good incident report separates the observation, the possible impact and the uncertainties. Use the authorised reporting channel; remove real secrets and unnecessary personal data from the report. Write down which source was examined and when. Presenting an unconfirmed assumption as fact can trigger the wrong response and unnecessary data sharing.

## Synthetic evidence

```text
Observed: unauthorized share confirmed
Unknown: access to other files
Contact: designated incident owner
```

## Investigation steps

1. Write the verified finding
2. Limit uncertainty and impact
3. Report through the authorised channel with the minimum data

Key term: **report**.

## Remember

- Verified findings and uncertainty should be written separately.
- Keep observations separate from assumptions.
- Record evidence, scope, and the reason for the next step.
