# How do we preserve context when searching logs?

> Editorial draft. Technical and language review are pending; this is not published pilot content.

## The case

Filtering by a keyword narrows a large record, but it can also hide important neighbouring lines. Preserve the source file, record the filter and check the results again against a time range. A word you cannot find does not prove that the event never happened; the scope of the record or a different wording may be the reason.

## Synthetic evidence

```text
09:01 login failed
09:02 login success
09:03 file shared
Filter: failed
```

## Investigation steps

1. Preserve the source record
2. Apply and record the filter
3. Compare neighbouring events over time

Key term: **grep**.

## Remember

- The filter result is not the whole source record.
- Keep observations separate from assumptions.
- Record the evidence, the scope and the reason for the next step.
