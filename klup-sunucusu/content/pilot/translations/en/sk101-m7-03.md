# Which evidence do we preserve during initial response?

> Editorial draft. Technical and language review are pending. This is not published pilot content.

## The case

After a suspicious attachment is opened, the goal is to limit the impact and keep an understandable record of the incident. Within the authorised process, restrict the relevant device or session; preserve the original logs. Deleting files carelessly or changing the system can make the investigation harder. Record the time, reason and owner of every step.

## Synthetic evidence

```text
09:10 attachment opened
09:12 unexpected login confirmed
09:14 incident owner notified
```

## Investigation steps

1. Identify the authorised incident owner
2. Limit impact and preserve evidence
3. Record actions on a timeline

Key term: **containment**.

## Remember

- Containment and evidence preservation should be planned together.
- Keep observations separate from assumptions.
- Record evidence, scope, and the reason for the next step.
