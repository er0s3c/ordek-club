# Which boundary do we check before reconnaissance?

> Editorial draft. Technical and language review are pending; this is not published pilot content.

## The case

A scan tool's result is an observation for the method and conditions used, and nothing more. No response does not mean the device is definitely switched off. Even in the Club lab, verify the permitted target, the scanning window and the limit of impact. The evidence in this lesson is synthetic; the example addresses given are not to be used as real targets.

## Synthetic evidence

```text
Target: 192.0.2.40
Observation: no response
Scope: offline training evidence only
```

## Investigation steps

1. Verify the scope
2. Record the observation conditions
3. Evaluate alternative explanations

Key term: **reconnaissance**.

## Remember

- More than one explanation for a lack of response must be investigated.
- Keep observations separate from assumptions.
- Record the evidence, the scope and the reason for the next step.
