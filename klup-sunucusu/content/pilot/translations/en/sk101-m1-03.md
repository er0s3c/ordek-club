# How do we connect the stages of an incident?

> Editorial draft. Technical and language review are pending; this is not published pilot content.

## The case

A single alert is not the whole story of an attack. At Mavi Fener, a sent message, the sign-in that follows and a file share can be linked on a timeline. Keep the account, device and time context while you build the link. Seeing the stages together does not, on its own, prove that every action was carried out by the same person.

## Synthetic evidence

```text
08:40 message received
08:43 account=events login=success
08:44 account=events share created
```

## Investigation steps

1. Align the timestamps
2. Identify shared accounts and devices
3. Describe the supported link and the uncertainties

Key term: **timeline**.

## Remember

- Events must be linked with time and identity context.
- Keep observations separate from assumptions.
- Record the evidence, the scope and the reason for the next step.
