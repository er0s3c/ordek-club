# How do you apply least privilege in everyday work?

> Editorial draft. Technical and language review are pending. This is not published pilot content.

## The case

A service account that reads reports may not need to create users. Permissions should be limited by business need, resource and time. Using a restricted account reduces the impact of a mistake or of abuse; monitoring and access review are still required. Testing the necessary functions before reducing permissions protects security and business continuity together.

## Synthetic evidence

```text
Task: read report
Current permissions: read, write, create-user
Required permission: read
```

## Investigation steps

1. Determine the business need
2. Map the required permissions
3. Verify the reduction with a functional test

Key term: **least privilege**.

## Remember

- Permissions outside the business need should be reviewed.
- Keep observations separate from assumptions.
- Record evidence, scope, and the reason for the next step.
