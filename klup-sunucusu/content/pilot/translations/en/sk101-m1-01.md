# What are we protecting?

> Editorial draft. Technical and language review are pending; this is not published pilot content.

## The case

Changing a port shipping list is a security incident, even if the file still opens. Confidentiality, integrity and availability ask different questions. First identify the asset and its business purpose; then prove which property was damaged. A control that supports one goal does not automatically deliver the others.

## Synthetic evidence

```text
Original: container=MF-14 destination=North
Observed: container=MF-14 destination=South
File remains readable.
```

## Investigation steps

1. Identify the original record
2. Compare the records
3. Describe the change's impact

Key term: **integrity**.

## Remember

- The observed change concerns integrity.
- Keep observations separate from assumptions.
- Record the evidence, the scope and the reason for the next step.
