# Levels, assessment and certificate

> Your progress shows across four levels. Your mark comes not only from the exam but from **what you do**.

::cizgi[p-03-sahne]

## Four levels

| Level | What can you do? |
|---|---|
| 🛡️ **Cyber Aware** | You recognise digital threats, protect yourself and report anything suspicious |
| 💻 **Cyber Foundations** | You use the basics of Linux, networking and the web, and finish guided labs |
| 🔎 **Cyber Practitioner** | You test and analyse in your chosen field, verify your finding and report on it |
| 🏆 **Cyber Project Graduate** | You manage a security problem from start to finish and present your project |

> These levels are the academy's own stages; they are not an official professional qualification or an accredited certificate.

## Assessment

| Area | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Weekly short quizzes | 20% |
| Hands-on lab missions | 35% |
| Ethical process and technical reporting | 15% |
| Capstone project | 30% |

## To earn the certificate of achievement

- Attend at least **75%** of the course (your attendance in live lessons is recorded automatically)
- Complete the ethics and law module (M02)
- Score enough on the practice missions
- Submit your capstone project
- Follow the lab rules
