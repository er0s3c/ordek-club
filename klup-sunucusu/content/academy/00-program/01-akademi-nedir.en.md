# What is Ördek Academy?

> A 30-week path for students from **any** department who want to learn cyber security **from scratch**.
> You don't have to be a computer engineer — being curious is enough.

![The Shadow Network Family watching a lit campus building from a dark street, students studying in its windows](../gorseller/p-01-a.webp)

## How do we learn?

**Learn → Observe → Practise → Defend → Report**

In every topic you first see *why* it matters, then you try it with your own hands.
Understanding the attack is how you build the defence. Being able to explain what you found is part of the skill too.

## The programme has three parts

![A three-stage mountain trail: a wide shared path, a crossroads splitting into seven coloured routes, and a small spotlit stage at the summit](../gorseller/p-01-b.webp)

| Part | Week | What's there? |
|---|---|---|
| **I — Shared foundation** | 1–12 | For everyone: computers, Linux, networking, web, Python, cryptography, OSINT, types of attack, a mini CTF |
| **II — Specialisation** | 13–24 | Pick a path: Web, Network, SOC/DFIR, Threat Intelligence, Artificial Intelligence, Mobile/IoT, Reverse Engineering |
| **III — Capstone project** | 25–30 | A realistic case with your team, a report and Demo Day |

## Weekly pace

- **3 hours** of guided teaching (a club lesson or a live session)
- **2 hours** of practice on your own (Academy pages and labs)

You can complete any live lesson you missed in the Academy at your own pace.

## One promise

You use everything you learn here **only in authorised environments**:
your own computer, the club's lab, or systems you have written permission to work on.
Details: [Lab rules](04-laboratuvar-kurallari.md).
