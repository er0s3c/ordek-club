## The Shield's three rules

1. **No permission, no key press.** Lawyer Kemal is counting on exactly that.
2. **An attack is never answered with an attack.** The door is closed, the evidence is preserved, the report is written.
3. **Every file is a lesson.** You can't stop the villain without understanding the trick.

## Who is connected to whom?

- **Baron Eren** raised Don; his right hand is **Single-Quote Jasmine**, and her apprentice is **Puppeteer Orhan**.
- **Don Shadow**'s daughter is **Masked Mine**, his nephew is **Smooth-Talking Tevfik**, his right-hand man is **Ransom Ferhat**.
- **Lawyer Kemal** knows everyone's secret — but he secretly works for Mine.
- **Locksmith Rıza** and **Safecracker Kadir** came out of the same cell block.
- **Superuser Sadri** is the man who opened young Eren's first terminal.
- **Cookie-Baker Celal**'s bakery is the family's meeting place; his cousin **Listener Nuri** listens to the bakery's Wi-Fi.
