# TRNC campus scenarios

> The cases in the lessons resemble university life in the Turkish Republic of Northern Cyprus (TRNC), but they are **entirely fictional**.
> No real institution is imitated and no real personal data is used.

![A model of a fictional Mediterranean campus; red markers above six buildings and a member of the family standing at each one](../gorseller/p-07-a.webp)

| # | Scenario | Level | Topic |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | **Fake scholarship application** — a "You've won a scholarship!" message asks for your account details | Beginner | Phishing, OSINT |
| 02 | **The campus Wi-Fi mystery** — secure and insecure access points in a simulated network | Beginner/Intermediate | Wi-Fi, MITM |
| 03 | **The missing student file** — a broken access control in a fictional student system | Beginner/Intermediate | IDOR, authorization |
| 04 | **The suspicious dorm computer** — synthetic Windows logs and network records | Intermediate | SOC, digital forensics |
| 05 | **The fake rental listing** — the listing doesn't match open-source records | Beginner | OSINT, social engineering |
| 06 | **The smart campus** — simulated lighting, sensor and energy devices | Advanced | IoT, OT, risk |

## How do you work?

1. Read the case file and clarify **the question**.
2. Examine the evidence — everything is synthetic and in the lab.
3. Write down everything you find together with **why** you think so.
4. Finish with a prevention recommendation.

Scenarios 01 and 05 are waiting for you in the foundation part (M01, M09).
