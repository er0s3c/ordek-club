# Search and archives: where did the information come from?

> If five sites say the same thing, do you have five sources, or five echoes of one source?
> 🚩 **Flag 2:** Two news sites say "according to experts, MERCAN-7". When you follow the links, they all lead to the same anonymous blog.

::cizgi[d-02-sahne]

## Advanced search

```text
"MERCAN-7" site:example            exact phrase, single site
"Fog Whistle" -advert              exclude a word
filetype:pdf "threat report"       PDF only
"MERCAN-7" before:2026-10-04       before a specific date
```

The goal is not many results but the **oldest** result. The place where a claim first appears is usually the source of the story.

## Archives

Pages change and get deleted. The web archive (Wayback Machine — `web.archive.org`) shows past copies of a page:
was a blog post corrected later, was a site that looks "old" actually opened last week?
Archive your own finding too: evidence shouldn't disappear when the source does.

## Circular reporting

![Town criers spreading each other's copies form a ring; all of them trace back to a single gossiping ear](../../gorseller/d-02-b.webp)

A cites B; B cites C; C cites A again. In the end, a **single** claim looks as if it has many sources.
This is the cheapest amplifier of a false flag. Trace every link backwards, and order the timestamps.

## The Admiralty code

Rate the source and the information **separately**: a reliable source can be wrong, and a doubtful source can tell the truth.

| Source reliability | Information credibility |
|---|---|
| **A** Completely reliable | **1** Confirmed by another source |
| **B** Usually reliable | **2** Probably true |
| **C** Fairly reliable | **3** Possibly true |
| **D** Usually not reliable | **4** Doubtful |
| **E** Not reliable | **5** Improbable |
| **F** Cannot be judged | **6** Cannot be judged |

Example: an unverified claim on a newly opened anonymous blog → **F6**.

Order K2, K3, K4, K6 and K7 in `lab/d-tehdit-raporlari.md` **by their time**. Where did the "MERCAN-7" claim first appear?

## ✅ Remember

- The oldest result matters, not the most results.
- Five sites repeating the same claim can be one source.
- Source and information are rated separately: letter + number.
