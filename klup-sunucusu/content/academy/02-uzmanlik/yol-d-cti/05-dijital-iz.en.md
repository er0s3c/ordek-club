# Digital footprint and fake identities: who is talking?

> What looks like a crowd can be one man's twelve hats.
> 🚩 **Flag 5:** Two minutes after the blog post, twelve accounts share the same sentence, with the same typo.

::cizgi[d-05-sahne]

## Signs of a fake identity

| Sign | Example |
|---|---|
| **Age** | The account was opened a few days before the incident |
| **Face** | Flawless, but the ears, glasses or hairline are wrong (synthetic) or it is a stock photo |
| **Timing** | The same content within minutes, the same time zone |
| **Language** | Copy-pasted text, a shared typo |
| **Network** | The accounts always follow each other and the same small group |

A single sign proves nothing; **several together** point to coordinated inauthentic behaviour.

## Social engineering targets the researcher too

Bedri also baits analysts: a "leaked report" link, an account offering to be a "source".

::animasyon[phishing]

## The researcher's safety (OPSEC)

![A researcher watches from an observation booth behind a one-way mirror; their own shadow stays inside](../../gorseller/d-05-b.webp)

- Don't research from your personal account; use the lab browser or a separate virtual machine.
- Don't click a suspicious link; examine it through an archive or a sandbox.
- **Don't interact with real people** from a fake account; in the Academy, you only observe passively.

## Your own footprint

How does an attacker see you? Check your own digital footprint regularly:
search results for your name, public profiles, old usernames, locations in photos.

## 🧪 Try it (5 min)

1. `lab/d-tehdit-raporlari.md` → K3. How many of the signs in the table apply to `@gercek_analist_x`?
2. Search your own name in quotes: is there anything on the first two pages you **don't want** there? Review your privacy settings.

## ✅ Remember

- It isn't one sign but the combination of signs that gives away a fake crowd.
- The researcher is a target too: a separate environment, no personal account, think before you click.
- Examine your own digital footprint before an attacker does.
