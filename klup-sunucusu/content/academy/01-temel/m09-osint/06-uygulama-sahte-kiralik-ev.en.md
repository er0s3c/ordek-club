# Practice: The Fake Rental Advert

> **Scenario (KKTC-05):** Before the new term, Mert is looking for a flat in Kyrenia. One advert catches his eye.
> All people, adverts and records are **fictional**.

::cizgi[m09-06-sahne]

## The advert

> 🏡 **Central Kyrenia, 2 min to the sea, 2+1 furnished — €250 a month!** Suitable for students.
> The landlord is currently abroad and will send the key by courier. **If the deposit (2 months) is sent today** the flat is yours.
> WhatsApp only: +90 5xx … · Payment: international money transfer

## Extra information Mert found

![The same villa photo pinned on a map in two different countries on a fake listing board](../../gorseller/m09-06-b.webp)

- The same photos turn up in a reverse search in a listing in another country, at a different price.
- Similar flats in the area rent for €550–700.
- The account that posted the advert was opened 5 days ago; it has no other adverts.
- His offer of a video call was answered with "my internet is bad".

## Mission

1. **Signs:** List the scam signs in the advert and the extra information (at least 5).
2. **Verification plan:** Design a **4-step** checklist Mert should follow before sending any money.
3. **Personal safety:** Which information should Mert **not share** with the advertiser?

<details><summary>Check</summary>Signs: a price far below the market · the landlord being "abroad" · the key by courier · an up-front and urgent deposit · an untraceable payment method · messaging only, avoiding a video call · stolen photos · a new account. Verification: see the flat in person (or have someone trustworthy see it) · match the identity against the title deed/lease · confirm through a known estate agency or the university housing office · pay only against a contract and handover of the key, by a traceable method. Not to be shared: a photo of ID/passport, bank details, student number.</details>

## ✅ Remember

- Very cheap + in a hurry + a landlord far away = the classic pattern.
- No money is sent without seeing the flat and without a contract.
- A photo of your ID is not something to hand to an advertiser.
