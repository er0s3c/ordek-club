# Is public information free for everyone to use?

> **OSINT** (Open-Source Intelligence) is the art of gathering information from public sources and **verifying** it.
> It is a powerful tool — and that is exactly why it has limits.

::cizgi[m09-01-sahne]

> 🕴️ **FILE: Masked Mine** · *Information Broker*
>
> ![Masked Mine — Information Broker](../../gorseller/karakter-mine.webp)
>
> **Threat:** Collecting personal information (doxxing), fake profiles, fake listings. · [Shadow Network story →](../../00-program/09-golge-ag.md)

::cizgi[mine-acilis]

## Where is it used?

- To work out **whether a piece of news, an image or a listing is true**
- To find out what an organisation shows about itself on the internet (attack surface, Path A/B)
- To expose scams and fake profiles
- Threat intelligence (Path D)

## Public ≠ free to use

![Someone gathering armfuls of books from an open shelf, next to a student taking only the one book they need](../../gorseller/m09-01-b.webp)

Just because information can be found does not mean it is right to **collect it, combine it and share it**.

| ✅ Ethical OSINT | ❌ Not ethical |
|---|---|
| Fictional cases, authorised targets | Building a profile of a classmate |
| Analysing an organisation's outside view with its own permission | Finding someone's home address and routine |
| Verifying a listing or a news item | Combining what you find and publishing it (doxxing) |
| Reporting a fake account | Opening a fake account and approaching someone |

## Three rules

1. **Write down the purpose and scope.** "What am I researching, and why?" If you have no answer, don't start.
2. **Data minimisation.** Collect only as much as you need to answer the question; don't keep the rest.
3. **Protect yourself.** Don't research from your personal account; avoid actions that leave a trace (e.g. browsing sites that show profile visits).

In this Academy, OSINT work is done **only on fictional people and organisations**.

## 🧪 Try it (3 min)

"I found the Instagram account, home address and car of a shop owner on campus" — which rules does this sentence break?

## ✅ Remember

- OSINT = gathering public sources **and verifying them**.
- Being public doesn't mean collecting and sharing it is ethical.
- Write the purpose, collect little, protect yourself.
