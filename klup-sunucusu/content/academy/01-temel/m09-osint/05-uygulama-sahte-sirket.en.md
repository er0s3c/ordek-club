# Practice: Find the Fake Company

> **Case:** An internship advert on the campus board: *"NovaKıbrıs Software Ltd. — looking for 10 interns, €900 a month.
> Application fee €50."* Club members ask you whether the advert is trustworthy.
> All of the records below are **fictional**.

::cizgi[m09-05-sahne]

## Evidence file

**E1 — Advert text:** "15 years of experience · head office in Nicosia · applications to ik.novakibris@mail.example"

**E2 — WHOIS (`novakibris.example`):** Registration date: 18 days ago · Registrant: privacy service

**E3 — Website:** 6 photos on the "Our team" page. In a reverse image search, 4 of them turn up on a stock photo site.

**E4 — Web archive:** No records at all for the domain name.

**E5 — Company register (fictional registry):** No record found under the name "NovaKıbrıs Software"; there is a 2019 record named "Nova Kıbrıs Consulting", field of activity accounting.

**E6 — Social media:** The account was opened 3 weeks ago, 2,400 followers, 9 posts, all the comments say "Great opportunity!"

**E7 — Map:** The address given is a café.

## Mission

![Innocuous-looking cards lined up side by side on a desk join up into a drawing of a fake company building](../../gorseller/m09-05-b.webp)

1. Classify each piece of evidence as **supports / contradicts / unclear**.
2. List **at least 5 inconsistencies** between the advert's claims and the evidence.
3. Write your overall conclusion **with a level of confidence**.
4. Prepare a 3-point recommendation for the club members.

## Submission

The report skeleton from M09-04, no more than 1 page.

<details><summary>Check</summary>Inconsistencies: "15 years" ↔ an 18-day-old domain name and no trace in the archive · a corporate claim ↔ a free email service · team photos are stock · no such company in the register (the similar name is a different field) · the address is a café · high and apparently fake engagement on a new account. Most important: the <b>application fee</b> — legitimate employers don't take money from interns. Conclusion: very probably a scam, level of confidence high. Recommendation: don't pay, don't send personal documents, report the advert to the careers centre.</details>

## ✅ Remember

- Records that look harmless one by one tell a story when put side by side.
- Compare claims with independent records.
- "A fee to apply" is always a red flag.
