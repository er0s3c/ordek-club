# Hacking the human

> Breaking a system can be hard; **persuading someone** is often easier.
> Social engineering targets human psychology, not technology.

::cizgi[m10-03-sahne]

## Six triggers

![A person controlled by six puppet strings; each string is tied to a different emotion: a badge, an hourglass, a shadow of fear, a mysterious box, a helping hand and a bag of gold](../../gorseller/m10-03-b.webp)

| Trigger | Example sentence |
|---|---|
| **Authority** | "I'm calling from the Rector's Office." |
| **Urgency** | "If you don't confirm within 10 minutes, your account will be deleted." |
| **Fear** | "A suspicious sign-in was made from your account!" |
| **Curiosity** | "The file with the exam questions that leaked is attached." |
| **Helpfulness** | "I forgot my card, could you hold the door?" |
| **Greed** | "You've won a gift voucher." |

## The phishing family

- **Phishing** — Bulk, fake email.
- **Spear phishing** — Prepared specifically for the target (they know your name and your department).
- **Smishing / vishing** — By SMS / by phone.
- **Quishing** — A fake QR code (stuck over a poster).

## Defence: "stop, verify, report"

![Three steps: a student raising a palm to stop, the same student verifying by calling from a separate phone, and a student ringing a warning bell](../../gorseller/m10-03-c.webp)

1. **Stop** — If you feel urgency, that is a sign.
2. **Verify** — Confirm through a channel you know (the official site, a known phone number); don't use the link or number in the message.
3. **Report** — Even if you clicked, don't be ashamed, report it straight away; reporting early reduces the damage.

## ✅ Remember

- Social engineering targets the person, not the technology.
- Authority, urgency, fear, curiosity, helpfulness, greed.
- Stop, verify through an independent channel, report.
