# Practice: The Ethical Hacker Agreement

> **Scenario:** A security test is requested for the club events site of North Island University (fictional),
> `etkinlik.kuzeykiyi.edu.example`. The site is managed by the club's IT officer, Ms Ayşe.
> You will carry out the test. First, write the permission.

::cizgi[m02-06-sahne]

## Your mission

![Students at the club table taking turns signing a contract with a glowing green seal](../../gorseller/m02-06-b.webp)

Prepare an authorization document of **no more than 1 page** by filling in the template below.

```text
SECURITY TEST AUTHORIZATION DOCUMENT

1. Parties
   System owner:   ..................  Signature/Date: ........
   Tester:         ..................  Signature/Date: ........

2. Purpose: ...........................................

3. Scope (only these):
   - ...

4. Out of scope:
   - ...

5. Time period: ....................

6. Permitted methods:
   - ...

7. Prohibited activities:
   - ...

8. Data handling: How will the data obtained be stored, and when will it be deleted?

9. Contact and emergency stop: ..........

10. Reporting: To whom, when and in what format will the report be delivered?
```

## Assessment

| Criterion | Points |
|---|---:|
| Are the scope and out-of-scope items clear? | 3 |
| Are the prohibited activities realistic? | 2 |
| Are data handling and deletion defined? | 2 |
| Are emergency stop and contact present? | 2 |
| Is it short and clear? | 1 |

<details><summary>Hint</summary>List the student portal, the email system and the real accounts of club members as out of scope. Add denial of service and downloading real user data to the prohibited activities.</details>

## ✅ Remember

- The permission document is the first deliverable of the test.
- Out of scope is written as clearly as scope.
- How you delete the data matters as much as how you find it.
