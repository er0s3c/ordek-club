    tur: coktan
    secenekler:

    aciklama: A test carried out without written permission and a defined scope is unauthorised access, even if the intent is good.

    tur: dogru-yanlis
    dogru: false
    aciklama: Being public does not mean permission to test.
  - soru: During a test within scope, you notice a vulnerability on a system that is out of scope. Which is the right thing to do?
    tur: coktan
    secenekler:
      - Quickly verify it and add it to the report
      - Note it without touching it and report it to the contact
      - Ignore it and tell no one
      - Share it on social media
    dogru: 1
    aciklama: Out-of-scope systems are not touched; the observation is reported and the decision is left to the system owner.
  - soru: What is the first step in responsible disclosure?
    tur: coktan
    secenekler:
      - Downloading data to prove the vulnerability
      - Stopping further use of the vulnerability
      - Publishing a blog post immediately
      - Offering the organisation a ransom
    dogru: 1
    aciklama: Stop first; continuing to use the vulnerability causes harm and legal risk.
  - soru: What does the principle of "minimisation" mean in personal data processing?
    tur: coktan
    secenekler:
      - Storing data in small files
      - Collecting only as much data as is necessary for the purpose
      - Compressing the data
      - Keeping the data on a small number of servers
    dogru: 1
    aciklama: Data that isn't necessary isn't collected; data that isn't collected can't leak.
  - soru: Pasting real user logs into an AI chat tool for analysis is a safe practice.

    dogru: false
    aciklama: The data would be sent to a third party; it should be masked first and the organisation's policy checked.
